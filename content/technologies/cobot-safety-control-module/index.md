## Problem

Collaborative robots still often rely on conservative, fixed safety limits or physical cages, which reduce throughput and limit true human-robot collaboration.

## Solution & USP

A control module that senses the proximity and motion of nearby people and continuously adjusts the robot's speed and force in real time, keeping operation within safety limits while preserving productivity. It is designed to integrate with existing cobot controllers.

## Applications & Use Cases

- Human-robot shared workstations in assembly and logistics
- Flexible small-batch manufacturing cells
- Retrofitting existing cobots for safer, faster collaboration

## Validation Status

Demonstrated on an in-house cobot test cell at TRL 6. Next step is integration and a PoC on a partner's robot platform and use case.

## Collaboration

Open to licensing the control module, joint development for a specific robot platform, and a PoC in a partner's production environment.
