## Problem

Early detection of key biomarkers often requires centralized lab equipment, trained staff, and long turnaround times, limiting point-of-care and resource-constrained settings.

## Solution & USP

A detection platform that identifies target biomarkers from a small sample volume with a simplified workflow and a short time-to-result. The chemistry is adaptable to multiple biomarker targets on the same platform.

## Applications & Use Cases

- Point-of-care screening in clinics and field settings
- Companion diagnostics for specific conditions
- Research-use assays for biomarker studies

## Validation Status

Validated in the lab against reference samples. Clinical validation and regulatory pathway assessment are the next milestones and are well suited to a partnered program.

## Collaboration

Seeking a diagnostics or medtech partner for co-development, clinical validation, and licensing of the underlying assay chemistry.
