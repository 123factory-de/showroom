## Problem

Volumetric video — true 3D capture of people and scenes — is compute-heavy and usually offline, which blocks live XR experiences, remote presence, and real-time virtual production.

## Solution & USP

A capture-and-processing pipeline that reconstructs volumetric video in real time from a multi-camera rig and streams it to XR devices or production tools with low latency. It is optimized to run on commodity GPUs.

## Applications & Use Cases

- Live XR and metaverse experiences
- Remote presence and telepresence
- Virtual production and broadcast media

## Validation Status

A working prototype runs on a multi-camera rig at TRL 5. The next step is scaling to live streaming and integrating with a partner's XR or production workflow.

## Collaboration

Seeking a media, XR, or broadcast partner for joint development, licensing of the processing pipeline, and a PoC on a target experience.
